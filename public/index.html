Scoring

Each team receives up to 80 points from form (15), venue strength (15), attack (15), defence (15), availability (10), motivation (5), and tactical fit (5). A quick filter then removes weak candidates. This is a ranking model—not a guarantee.

Data quality: injury counts are real API data when the league/date has injury coverage; motivation and tactical fit are still neutral placeholders. Do not treat a high score as a verified betting edge until the model has been walk-forward tested.
